## Why the separation matters

Flashing is unforgiving about detail. A ROM for the wrong model floats along happily and then leaves you with a black screen. A recovery flashed to one slot instead of both comes up on one boot and not the next. A wipe done after the flash instead of before turns a ten-minute job into a loop.

That is why the files and the written notes sit together, in the long videos, where there is room to explain the order. A few seconds of trend audio is not the place for a vendor image.
