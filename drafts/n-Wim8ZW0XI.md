## For anyone who came for the phone content

The flashing stuff has not gone anywhere. The toolkit is the same as it has
always been:

- The free Rom Flasher tool, for both the phone and the PC
- ADB and Fastboot platform files, so fastboot works on your machine at all
- The ADB and fastboot driver, so Windows stops showing an unknown device
- Recovery images and the files people forget before their first flash

Those all sit in the downloads listed with this video, and none of them cost
anything. If you need a recovery image, remember they are device specific. A
recovery built for one model is not going to work on another, no matter how
similar the names look.

One habit worth keeping, whatever you are flashing: back up first, and confirm
the file matches your exact model. A wrong file is how most dead phones happen.
The ROM rarely does the damage.
