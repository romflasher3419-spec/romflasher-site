## Why some things get a short answer

A few of the questions I get have a short correct answer, and stretching them
into a ten minute video would just waste your time.

- "Do I have to unlock the bootloader first?" Yes. Every method here assumes it,
  and unlocking wipes the phone, so it happens before anything else.
- "Will a custom ROM fix my battery?" Sometimes, but not always. A bad build can
  drain faster than stock, and the only honest test is a few days of real use.
- "Is the tool safe?" I use the same free Rom Flasher tool on my own phone. What
  matters is that you got it from the link in the downloads, not a random mirror.

Those are the kind of answers that fit in a reply. They do not need an intro, a
sponsor read or a three-minute monologue.

## What the channel looks like day to day

Two things live here. The long videos are the work: unlocking, flashing, chasing
down why a phone will not boot, then filming the fix properly. The short side is
the everyday stuff. Quick opinions, quick replies, and the occasional answer to
whoever wrote in first.

I do not plan the shorts much. If something is worth saying and it will fit in
under a minute, it gets said.

## What is missing from this one

No device on the desk. No ROM zip. No benchmark numbers, and no "this phone beats
that phone" claims that would only hold until the next build. Those belong in the
dedicated videos, and those are where you should look if you are deciding
something with money on the line.

So if you are here for a step-by-step, go to one of the install videos. This one
is just the conversation.
