Putting another brand's interface on a POCO is always a fun experiment, and this one mostly delivered. REALME UI 16.0.7 is an official Android 16 build put together for the POCO X7 Pro, and I installed it with the PC tool, then went through the look, the features and the bugs worth knowing about.

If you would rather flash straight from the phone, there is a Termux route linked in the downloads section. Same ROM, different hands.

## What it looks like

Unmistakably Realme. The icons are bold and rounded, the clock widgets are front and centre, and the whole thing has a look that does not try to be stock Android. Animations feel deliberate rather than fast, and the launcher has its own way of grouping things.

The bundled apps are the reason to bother. There is a Realme dialer with call recording built in, a file manager, and the widget set you would expect from Realme. If you have used a Realme phone before, you will know where everything is within a few minutes.

## What was tested

- A clean install and the first boot behaviour.
- The look: icons, widgets, animations and the launcher.
- The camera and the bundled Realme apps.
- The phone dialer, call recording and the file manager.
- Google Play certification and basic integrity on my run.
- Stability over the first day of use.

## What works

Calls work, and that includes call recording, which is the feature people usually ask about first. The camera is usable in normal light. The Realme apps that come bundled all opened and behaved like they should, and the file manager is genuinely nicer than what you get stock in a lot of ROMs. Google Play certification came through in my run, so the store and basic integrity were fine on this install.

## What is broken

This is a port, so be careful with what you expect. Some apps behaved differently in my test, and integrity results vary app by app. That last part is normal for a ported ROM and not a sign that you did something wrong.

The realistic problem is banking and payment apps. Some of them look at the system in ways a port will not satisfy, and they may refuse to run no matter what you do. Do not move your phone over to this build expecting your bank app to work first time.

## Who should install it

Install it if you like the Realme look and you want calls, a camera and a file manager that work, and you are happy to keep banking on another device or keep a stock backup ready. If your phone is your main tool for payments and work apps, this is not the ROM to move to permanently.

## What you need

- An unlocked bootloader and fastboot access on the POCO X7 Pro.
- The REALME UI 16.0.7 package for Rodin, from the link in the downloads section.
- A PC with working ADB and fastboot, or the Termux route if you prefer.
- A full backup. A clean install wipes the phone.
- Over 60% battery, and patience for a slow first boot.
